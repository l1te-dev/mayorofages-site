Mayor of Ages — Your Ad Privacy Choices

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Mayor of Ages shows ads from Google AdMob. You can change how your data is used for ads at any time. The game works the same whatever you choose.

In the game

Tap the Leader (crown) button → Settings → Ad privacy options. This reopens Google's consent message, where you can accept, reject, or change your choices, including opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information under US state privacy laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA). The button appears in regions where these choices apply.

On your iPhone

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track", or turn it off just for Mayor of Ages. Without tracking permission, ads are not personalized using your activity in other apps and websites.

With Google

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and manage your Google ad settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and My Ad Center.
